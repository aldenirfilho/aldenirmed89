Total Football operating model

The 1974 reference means coordinated rotations and intelligent occupation of space—not eleven people doing everything at once. In this interface, evidence, decisions, imaging, clinical application, active recall, and assessment cover one another without losing clear ownership.

Coverage Sources and review protect decisions. Mobility Each module supports the next action. Width The live map prevents isolated knowledge. Finishing Questions turn study into performance.
